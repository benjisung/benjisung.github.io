Portfolio / Your information

Visitor privacy.

A small log of how this portfolio is explored, with access kept private.

What is recorded

This portfolio records your public IP address, visit time, the portfolio page opened, and an approximate country code. Benjamin Sung uses these records to understand portfolio traffic. An IP address identifies a network connection and can be shared by several people.

Who can see it

Records are stored with Cloudflare and viewed through a private dashboard secured by Benjamin’s private access key. Visitor records are not included in this website’s public GitHub repository.

How long it stays

The dashboard covers the previous 30 days. Expired database records are deleted daily. Cloudflare’s infrastructure logs and backup retention are separate from this visitor log.

Your browser choices

The portfolio logger uses no tracking cookies or persistent visitor identifiers. It sends no query strings, page fragments, or referrer URLs. Collection is skipped when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.
