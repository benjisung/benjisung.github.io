My robotics work combines mechanical assembly, embedded control, computing, sensing, and repeated testing. The goal is to make software and hardware behave as one system.

INDEPENDENT PROJECTIN DEVELOPMENT

Autonomous Mobile Robot Development Platform

24V differential drive · STM32 low-level control · Jetson Orin Nano · ROS 2 Humble

I assembled this two-wheel differential-drive platform as a reusable base for robotics development. It began from one application idea, but the project has evolved into a general platform that can accept different sensors, perception models, and mechanisms.

The current work centers on hardware-software integration: power, geared motors, encoder feedback, embedded control, high-level Jetson compute, and ROS 2 communication. The robot exists and can be driven; the advanced navigation and perception stack is still being integrated and tested.

WHY_THIS_PROJECT_MATTERS

The platform gives me one system on which to learn mechanical design, power and motor integration, encoder feedback, embedded programming, Linux deployment, ROS 2, perception, and autonomy. It is intentionally not tied to a single end use.

02_VEX_ROBOTICS

PROGRAMMER / BUILDERWEGO ROBOTICS

Competition robotics under real constraints

In VEX Robotics, I worked across programming and building. I used C++ and PROS for robot programming and contributed to mechanism assembly and modification. My technical experience includes PID, odometry, repeated testing, tuning, and engineering design iteration.

Competition work taught me that an idea is only useful when the mechanism, code, timing, and driver needs all work together reliably.

Team recognition: VEX Create Award and VEX Sportsmanship Award.
