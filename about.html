// ENVIRONMENTAL + MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

I am a Grade 12 student who likes understanding how physical systems work and then building, testing, and improving them. Programming first taught me to break complex problems into smaller parts; robotics showed me that code also has to work with motors, mechanisms, sensors, power, and real-world uncertainty.

That interest expanded through SDG engineering projects and marine-conservation experiences. At NMMBA and HIMB, I saw how environmental science can connect with sensing, mapping, AI, and engineering. I now apply those ideas in an independent fish-detection system and an autonomous mobile robot development platform.

I am especially interested in environmental and mechanical engineering, with robotics and computer vision as tools for measuring and interacting with the physical world.
