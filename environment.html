How the journey began +

I independently prepared my application and proposal for the Taiwan Global Pathfinders Initiative Program (青年百億海外圓夢基金計畫). Approximately NT$280,000 in government funding supported preparatory learning in Taiwan and the completed overseas program. This was field learning and participation, rather than original conservation research.
